SwoleMate · Aesthetic Studies · 06 Directions + 03 Variants

Six ways the app could feel.

Each mockup shows the same moment — mid-session, set four of bench press, one PR landed, rest clock ticking — rendered in a different aesthetic. Designed for a phone, one-handed, with the active session as the emotional core.

▸ Direction 01 i.

Industrial chronograph

Dark, dense, mono-spaced numerics. A stopwatch on your wrist. The timer is loud, sets read like a data log, the rest clock looks like a piece of equipment.

Brutalist gym

Japanese grid

Strict hairline grid, generous negative space, vertical kana down the spine. PRs get a vermillion hanko seal. Mono numbers, serif headings, the quiet of a tea-room.
